# Pokopay Merchant — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 10 September 2026
**Applies to:** the Pokopay Merchant mobile app for iOS and Android ("the app"), operated by Pokopay ("we", "us").

## 1. What the app is for

The app lets Pokopay merchants view their sales, settlements, payouts, and business details. It reads data that already exists in your Pokopay merchant account. It does not process card payments itself.

## 2. Information we collect

**Account information you provide.** Your email address and password when you sign in. If you change your password in the app, the new password is sent to our servers over an encrypted connection and is never stored on the device.

**Business information from your Pokopay account.** Business name, merchant ID, contact details, address, terminal IDs, sales totals, settlement records, and bank account details used for payouts (account number is masked where shown). This information is retrieved from Pokopay's servers when you use the app.

**Device and diagnostic information.** App version, operating system version, device model, and, if a crash occurs, a technical crash report. Crash reports do not include your password, card numbers, or the content of your transactions.

**Biometric sign-in.** If you turn on biometric sign-in, the app asks your phone's operating system to confirm your fingerprint or face. The app never receives or stores your biometric data. To make biometric sign-in work, your sign-in credentials are stored in the device's secure storage (iOS Keychain or Android Keystore) and are removed when you turn biometric sign-in off or sign out.

**Preferences.** Settings such as report e-mail toggles are stored on your device.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers, and the app contains no advertising.

## 3. How we use information

- To sign you in and keep you signed in securely.
- To show your sales, settlements, payouts, and business details.
- To keep a short-lived local copy of your most recent figures so the app works on a poor connection.
- To find and fix crashes and errors.
- To tell you when an app update is required.

## 4. Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run the app, under contract and only for the purposes above:

- **Crash reporting** (Sentry). Receives crash reports and diagnostic information as described in section 2.
- **Cloud hosting** for Pokopay's own servers.

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect Pokopay, our merchants, or the public.

## 5. Retention

Data cached on your device is removed when you sign out or uninstall the app. Account and transaction records are retained on Pokopay's servers in line with our merchant agreement and Nigerian financial regulations. Crash reports are retained for up to 90 days.

## 6. Security

All traffic between the app and our servers uses TLS encryption. Session tokens are stored in the device's secure storage. You can sign out at any time from Settings, which removes all tokens and cached data from the device.

## 7. Your choices and rights

You can view and correct your business details by contacting support. You can turn biometric sign-in off in the app. Subject to applicable law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing support@pokopayng.com.

## 8. Children

The app is for business use and is not directed at children under 18.

## 9. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy in the app and on our website, and update the effective date above.
