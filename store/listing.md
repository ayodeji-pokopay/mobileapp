## Full description

Pokopay Merchant puts your business in your pocket. Sign in with the same account you use for your Pokopay card machine and see, at a glance, how today is going and when your money lands.

**Today at a glance**
See gross sales today, how many transactions you've taken, and what is waiting to be settled, right on the home screen.

**Sales reports**
Switch between today, last week, last month, and last year. See total sales, fees, and net amount, a daily sales chart, and a breakdown by card brand.

**Settlements and payouts**
Every settlement to your bank, with reference, date, transaction count, fees, and net amount. Tap any settlement for the full breakdown including the bank account it was paid to.

**Wallet**
Your balance, pending amount, and a payouts and fees history in one place.

**Your stores and card machines**
Business details and the terminal IDs assigned to you.

**Secure by design**
Sign in with your email and password, then unlock with Face ID, Touch ID, or your phone's fingerprint sensor. Sessions are protected with bank-grade encryption.

**Works when the network doesn't**
The app keeps your last synced figures so you can still check your numbers on a poor connection, and refreshes automatically when you are back online.

Pokopay Merchant is for businesses that accept payments with Pokopay. If you don't have a Pokopay account yet, visit pokopayng.com to get started.
